## 2026-05-08 - Add missing password toggle on reset form
**Learning:** Found that while the main Login/Signup form and the Update Password form had password visibility toggles, the Reset Password form (accessed via emailed link) did not. This creates a high-friction scenario since users are resetting their forgotten passwords and cannot verify they typed the new one correctly, leading to immediate lockouts if they make a typo.
**Action:** When auditing authentication flows, ensure password visibility toggles are applied uniformly across *all* password inputs (login, signup, update, and reset), not just the primary ones.
